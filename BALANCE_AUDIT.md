# Balance notes

The PDF baselines are treated as power budgets, not strict formulas. Special utility can trade raw damage/healing/armor for card advantage, turn control, protection, delayed effects, or setup.

## Rough targets
- 3★: around 30 damage / 40 healing / 30 armor worth of impact
- 4★: around 55–70 damage / ~70 healing / ~60 armor worth of impact
- 5★: around 100+ raw-stat impact or a major rule-changing effect

## Additional rules used here
- Flat combat values use multiples of 10.
- Turn counts, card counts, multipliers, and percentages do not need to be multiples of 10.
- Full turn skips, HP swaps, extra card plays, and reaction negation are valued much higher than their raw numbers suggest.
- Generated cards are normally limited to three meaningful effect clauses.
- Random filler that directly helps the opponent was removed.
- Field effects are intentionally small per turn because they can benefit multiple allied turns.

## Rarity decisions
- Boogie Woogie remains 5★ because HP swapping can create enormous swings.
- Sandevistan remains 5★ because it combines a skipped enemy turn with extra action economy.
- Quintessential Quintuplets is 5★ because it enables two additional non-5★ plays and does not consume the normal card play.
- Environment cards are 3★ and intentionally weak because they persist until replaced and are common in every deck.

## Environment balance update

Environment cards are intentionally common but individually weak. Each show has 6 Environment cards, all 3★. Their persistent benefit is only 10 points at a time (+10 attack, -10 incoming attack damage, +10 punch damage, +10 start-of-turn healing, or +10 start-of-turn armor). Only one Environment exists globally, so either team can overwrite the other team's Environment. A legal 48-card deck must include at least 9 Environment cards (3 from each selected show), creating frequent counterplay without making any single Environment dominate the match.
