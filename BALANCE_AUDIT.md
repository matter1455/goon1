# Balance notes

The original rarity baselines are treated as power budgets, not strict formulas. Special utility can trade raw damage/healing/armor for card advantage, turn control, protection, delayed effects, setup, or action economy.

## Rough targets
- 3★: around 30 damage / 40 healing / 30 armor worth of impact
- 4★: around 55–70 damage / ~70 healing / ~60 armor worth of impact
- 5★: around 100+ raw-stat impact or a major rule-changing effect

## Guardrails
- Flat combat values use multiples of 10.
- Turn counts, card counts, multipliers, and percentages do not need to be multiples of 10.
- Full turn skips, HP swaps, extra card plays, and reaction negation are valued much higher than their raw numbers suggest.
- Generated cards are normally limited to a few meaningful clauses.
- The larger 33-card show pools do **not** increase deck size or rarity quotas; they only increase deck-building choice.
- Generated effect text and executable structured abilities are checked for duplicates.

## Environment balance
Every show still has 6 Environment cards, all 3★. A legal 48-card deck includes at least 3 Environments from each selected show (9 total minimum). Only one Environment exists globally, so either team can overwrite the current one. Their persistent bonus stays at only 10 points per trigger.

## Rarity decisions already preserved
- Boogie Woogie is 5★ because HP swapping can create huge swings.
- Sandevistan is 5★ because it combines turn denial with extra action economy.
- Quintessential Quintuplets is 5★ because it breaks the normal one-card action limit.
- Environment cards stay 3★ because they are common and individually weak.
